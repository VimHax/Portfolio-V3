## The experience

Below are a few highlights of the website which define the experience.

### Airborn intelligence hub

<Video src="/video/demo.mp4"/>

When the user scrolls down they are they are presented with a guided experience, showcasing how an operator would be able to command the drones to accomplish a mission. This is a multi-stage animation which is paced by the user through their interactions.

### Capabilities

<Video src="/video/capabilities.mp4"/>

This section showcases the capabilities of the Warden. This is a scroll linked animation which fades in the capabilities one at a time whilst changing the hue of the scanning on the left.

### About us

<Video src="/video/physics.mp4"/>

The hero of the [About Us](https://akridia.com/about) page shows the names of the co-founders colliding and bouncing off of the edges of the hero section, the centered title and each other. This was accomplished with a small physics library that was configured to replicate the [bouncing DVD screensaver](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/DVD_screensaver) physics.
