I was brought on to this project to add decorations to really sell the
idea that this truly is a one of a kind party. To accomplish this I
worked on 2 effects, both heavily depend on GLSL shaders added to the
resource pack (which gets loaded by the client) but also a server-sided
plugin I wrote in Java.

This event needed to be readily accessible therefore client-sided
requirements needed to be minimized and various client configurations
needed to be considered such as Optifine, Sodium and Apple silicon to
name a few.
